3 · Track 1 — AI automation consulting (the banker)

Evidence: UK freelance AI rates £400–800/day; UK AI contract median £550/day (IT Jobs Watch, 3,957 vacancies); 54% UK SME AI adoption in 2026 with 60% citing a skills gap (BCC/YouGov). £5k = 8–12 billable days/year. Offshore platforms do undifferentiated automations for £120 — which is why the channel is warm network, never Upwork.

Positioning: not "AI consultant" — "I automate the back office of small product businesses — reconciliation, marketplace listings, social content — with systems I run in my own business." The live demos (statement reconciliation, eBay/Amazon listing automation, the Instagram pipeline) are the differentiator, backed by a 20-year Accenture delivery CV.

"Books that reconcile themselves" — bank/statement reconciliation automation£1,800
"Listings on autopilot" — marketplace listing/repricing automation£2,200

12-month shape: two gigs (≈£3.5–4k); convert one into a £300–400/mo "keep it running" retainer where natural — that alone is £4k/yr recurring. Channels: Accenture alumni, local business owners, the reseller community (resellers are SMEs with exactly these problems).

4 · Track 2 — Sniper in an adjacent niche (the recurring core)

Evidence: generic Vinted monitors charge £25–35/mo and sell (VINTREZ: ~1,680 members in year one at €14.90; Fyndit, Souk, AutoFlipped comparable); Whop + Discord is the standard channel (~7% all-in fees); closest extension precedent is Closet Tools (Poshmark) at a self-reported $42k/mo. Nobody found doing vision-ID + live margin calculation in the candidate niches. A free LEGO deal Discord (Brick Hound) holds 5,641 members; a US LEGO group (Brick Bucks) charges $57/mo with weaker tooling.

Design decisions (from the research)

  • Alert-only, no autobuy — Vinted's DataDome anti-bot partnership (Apr 2025) and the reported Feb 2026 ban wave make autobuy the liability tier; alert-plus-human-buys is the defensible side.
  • Per-user private filters/channels — shared feeds cannibalise deals and drive churn (the established tools all solve this the same way).
  • £12–15/mo on Whop, 7-day trial — deliberately under the £25–35 incumbents while offering more intelligence.
  • Costs: Keepa API €49/mo + vision API + proxies ≈ £80–100/mo at scale; net ≈ £3–4.5k/yr at 30–40 subs.

12-month shape: port (3–4 wks) → 5-user free beta in one community (4 wks) → Whop launch month 3 → 30–40 subs by months 9–12. Kill criterion: <15 subs at month 6 → sunset, redirect hours to the reserve track.

5 · Track 3 — the UK FIRE tool (the compounding bet)

Evidence of the gap: no UK CAPE-conditioned SWR tool exists anywhere; nothing integrates gilt ladders + SWR simulation + ISA/SIPP/allowance sequencing; the free incumbents (LateGenXer's Streamlit ladder builder, YieldGimp) are hobby-grade — cold-starts, no saved plans, no couples. Evidence of the moment: HL gilt trading +315% YoY with 25k+ clients holding gilts and record retail volumes (Feb 2025); r/FIREUK at 313k members (+14%/yr); Monevator paywalls its gilt-ladder spreadsheet at £3–8/mo — direct proof this audience pays for this exact content. US analogue: ProjectionLab, solo founder, $10k MRR.

Product: productise the engine already built for our own retirement plan — live-yield ILG ladder builder, the 155-year CAPE-SWR simulator, the wrapper-sequencing map. Free: single ladder, no save. Paid (£4–6/mo or £39/yr): saved plans, couples, scenarios, tax-sequencing report. Affiliate: ii/HL programmes pay £200–400/funded account — every ladder session naturally ends at "you need a broker that deals gilts"; 15–25 referrals/yr hits the target by itself.

FCA discipline: outputs framed as mathematical optimisation of user-specified constraints; education-only disclaimers; no steering to specific securities on specific platforms; affiliate links at platform level, never rung level. One-off legal sanity check (~£1–2k) before charging — funded from Track 1 proceeds. Precedent: LateGenXer, YieldGimp and EvolveMyRetirement all operate unauthorised with disclaimers; the FCA's 2026 "targeted support" reforms signal a friendlier climate for guidance-shaped tools.

12-month shape: free ladder tool live months 2–3 (the code exists — this is packaging) → build in public on r/FIREUK and Monevator comments → paywall + affiliates months 4–6 → £1–3k year 1, with the year-2 adviser-tier upside (Timeline charges advisers ~£400/seat/yr) as the reason it earns the slot.
