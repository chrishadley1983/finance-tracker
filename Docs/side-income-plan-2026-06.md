## 2. Track 1 — AI automation consulting (the banker)

**Evidence:** UK freelance AI rates £400–800/day, UK AI contract median £550/day ([IT Jobs Watch, 3,957 vacancies](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/artificial%20intelligence.do)); 54% UK SME AI adoption with 60% citing a skills gap (BCC/YouGov 2026). £5k = 8–12 billable days/year.

**Positioning:** not "AI consultant" (commodity) — *"I automate the back office of small product businesses — bookkeeping reconciliation, marketplace listings, social content — with working systems I run in my own business."* The demo portfolio is the differentiator: live statement-reconciliation, eBay/Amazon listing automation, the Instagram pipeline. Offshore platform gigs charge £120; your anchor is £1,500–2,500 fixed-scope with an Accenture CV behind it.

**Channel:** warm network only — Accenture alumni, local business owners, the reseller community (resellers are SMEs with exactly these problems). No Upwork (race to the bottom).

**Packages (publish these three, fixed price):**
1. *Books that reconcile themselves* — bank/statement reconciliation automation: £1,800
2. *Listings on autopilot* — marketplace listing/repricing automation: £2,200
3. *A month of content in an afternoon* — AI social pipeline setup + handover: £1,500

**12-month shape:** 2 gigs (≈£3.5–4k). Convert one into a £300–400/mo "keep it running" retainer if natural → that alone is £4k/yr recurring.

## 3. Track 2 — Sniper in an adjacent niche (the recurring core)

**Evidence:** generic Vinted monitors charge £25–35/mo and sell (VINTREZ ~1,680 members in year one at €14.90; Fyndit, Souk, AutoFlipped £25/mo); Whop+Discord is the standard channel (~7% all-in fees); the reseller-extension precedent is Closet Tools at $42k/mo (Poshmark). Nobody found doing vision-ID + live margin calc in the niches below.

**Niche selection (decide in week 2 — criteria: item identifiable from photos, liquid price reference, active Vinted supply, no HB conflict):**

| Candidate | Price reference | Vision-ID difficulty | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retro/boxed video games UK** | PriceCharting API | Easy (title text) | Strong flipper community; recommended first test |
| **LEGO on Vinted DE/FR → Amazon.de/fr** | Keepa (same API, different domain) | Zero work (same model) | Easiest port; mild HB overlap only if EU buyers ship to UK |
| Board games | BGG + eBay comps | Easy (box art) | Smaller flipper scene |
| Pokémon/TCG sealed | PriceCharting/TCGplayer | Moderate (sealed only; singles too hard) | Biggest market, most competition |

**Design decisions from the research:** alert-only, no autobuy (stays the safe side of Vinted enforcement — DataDome partnership and Feb 2026 ban wave make autobuy a liability); per-user private filters/channels (shared feeds cannibalise deals → churn); £12–15/mo on Whop with 7-day trial; costs ≈ Keepa €49/mo + vision API + proxies ≈ £80–100/mo at scale.

**12-month shape:** port (3–4 wks) → 5-user free beta inside one community (4 wks) → Whop launch month 3 → 30–40 subs by month 9–12 = £4.3–6.5k gross, **~£3–4.5k net**. Kill criterion: <15 subs at month 6 → sunset, redirect hours to Track 4.

## 4. Track 3 — the UK FIRE tool (the compounding bet)

**Evidence:** confirmed gap — no UK CAPE-conditioned SWR tool exists, nothing integrates gilt ladders + SWR + ISA/SIPP sequencing; incumbents are free but hobby-grade (Streamlit cold-starts). Demand moment is real: HL gilt trading +315% YoY, 25k+ clients holding gilts, record retail volumes Feb 2025; r/FIREUK = 313k members (+14%/yr); Monevator paywalls its ladder spreadsheet at £3–8/mo (proof this audience pays); ProjectionLab (US solo-dev analogue) hit $10k MRR.

**Product:** productise what we built for ourselves this week — live-yield ILG ladder builder (input spend + years → rung list + costs), the 155-year CAPE-SWR simulator, the wrapper-sequencing map. **Free:** single ladder, no save. **Paid (£4–6/mo or £39/yr):** saved plans, couples, scenarios, tax-sequencing report. **Affiliate:** ii/HL programmes exist (£200–400/funded account economics) — "you'll need a broker that deals gilts" is the natural end of every session.

**FCA discipline (from the research):** outputs = mathematical optimisation of user-specified constraints, education-only disclaimers, no steering to specific securities on specific platforms, affiliate links at platform level not rung level. One-off legal sanity check (~£1–2k) before charging — fund it from Track 1 proceeds.

**12-month shape:** free version live month 2–3 (the code exists; this is packaging) → build in public on r/FIREUK + Monevator comments → paywall + affiliates months 4–6 → realistic £1–3k yr-1, with the yr-2 upside (adviser tier, à la Timeline) being why it's worth the slot.

## 6. Risks and honest caveats

1. **The real bottleneck is selling, not building** — every research thread converged on this. The 90-day plan front-loads outreach (20 conversations before any new code beyond the niche port).
2. **Sniper track sits on ToS-violating access** (Vinted prohibits automation; DataDome anti-bot escalation is real). Alert-only + browser-side keeps it defensible; budget for the arms race and treat revenue as 2–4 year, not forever.
3. **Cannibalisation is designed out** (adjacent niches), but watch for drift — a "LEGO-EU" port leaks intelligence into your market faster than retro games would.
4. **FIRE tool revenue is the least certain** (frugal audience, free incumbents) — it's in the portfolio for asymmetry and personal fit, not certainty. Affiliates may out-earn subscriptions.
5. **Tax:** this income lands on top of HB profits in self-assessment; consulting VAT-free under the threshold; Whop/LemonSqueezy handle EU VAT as merchant-of-record. Keep tracks as separate income lines from HB in the books.
6. **Unverified research items:** r/legoinvest size (Reddit blocked), exact EvolveMyRetirement pricing, Monevator subscriber counts, all self-reported revenue claims (ShipFast, Closet Tools). None are load-bearing for the plan's maths.
