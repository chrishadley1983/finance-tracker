Reading it: from expensive markets like today’s, the risk is a poor first decade. In the worst tenth of history the pot falls to £1.31M by 2045, then recovers to £2.09M. That first decade is exactly what the gilts pay for, so we never have to sell shares at the bottom. Played forward 49 years from each of 1,274 starting points in US stock-market history since 1871, the plan ran out in 0% of them. What we gave up: in a long boom an all-shares plan would leave more. We chose certainty in the years that matter over the best case.

4 · Worth knowing

If one of us dies

ISAs can pass to the other with their tax-free wrapper. Pensions go to whoever is named on the nomination forms, which we are refreshing on every pot.

Not counted, on purpose

The house, any inheritance, and state pension rises above inflation. All of it is extra safety on top of these numbers.

Tax

Each pension is drawn up to the top of the basic-rate band once it opens, which keeps the tax low and leaves less for inheritance tax (pensions count towards it from 2027).

It checks itself

The plan re-runs every quarter against our real balances and spending, and flags anything drifting.

When we would revisit

A job change, health, spending stuck above the line, or a big market move either way. Later-life options (an annuity, or more gilts beyond 2045) are for around 2040.
