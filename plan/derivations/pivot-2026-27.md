# Pivot programme, hand derivation (August 2026 payslip basis)

Inputs (plan/assumptions.json): basic £73,837.80/yr; bonus 5% of basic; car allowance £6,500; medical BIK £1,330;
existing employee sacrifice 4.5% of basic; pay growth 2%/yr; modelled target ANI £60,000.

## Year 1, 2026/27

- Package = 73,837.80 + 3,691.89 + 6,500 + 1,330 = 85,359.69
- Existing sacrifice = 4.5% × 73,837.80 = 3,322.70
- ANI before = 85,359.69 − 3,322.70 = **82,036.99**
- Extra sacrifice to £60,000 = **22,036.99** → golden 22,037 (tol 1)
- Take-home cut at 42% relief (all above £50,270): 22,037 × 0.58 = 12,781

## Year 9, 2034/35

- Growth factor 1.02^8 = 1.171659; basic = 86,513.06
- ANI before = 86,513.06 × (1 + 0.05 − 0.045) + 6,500 + 1,330 = 86,945.63 + 7,830 = 94,775.63
- Extra = **34,775.63** → golden 34,776 (tol 2)

## Nine-year total

- Σ extra = 1.005 × 73,837.80 × Σ_{i=0..8} 1.02^i − 9 × (60,000 − 7,830)
- Σ 1.02^i (i = 0..8) = (1.02^9 − 1)/0.02 = (1.195093 − 1)/0.02 = 9.75463
- = 74,207.0 × 9.75463 − 469,530 = 723,862 − 469,530 = **254,332** → golden 254,330 (tol 15)

## Take-home in year 1 at a £60,000 ANI landing

After sacrifice, cash pay = 60,000 − 1,330 (BIK is in ANI but not cash) = 58,670; taxable = 60,000.

- Tax = 20% × 37,700 + 40% × (60,000 − 11,179 − 37,700) = 7,540 + 4,448.40 = 11,988.40
- NI = 3,016 + 2% × (58,670 − 50,270) = 3,016 + 168 = 3,184
- Net = 58,670 − 11,988.40 − 3,184 = **43,497.60** → golden 43,498 (tol 5); Amendment 1's "£43.5k" and "£16.5k of
  deductions" (11,988 + 3,184 + 1,330 medical = 16,502) both hold. Independent of the basic, by construction.
