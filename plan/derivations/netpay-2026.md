# Net pay reconciliation, April and August 2026 payslips

Rules 2026/27: personal allowance £12,570 but Abby's code is **1117L** (allowance £11,179; the payrolled medical
is also coded out), basic rate 20% on the first £37,700 of taxable income above the allowance, 40% above that;
employee NI 8% between £12,570 and £50,270, 2% above. NI is charged on cash pay; income tax on cash pay plus the
payrolled medical benefit. All thresholds frozen to April 2031.

## April 2026 (basic £5,825.00/mo)

- Cash pay = 5,825 × (1 − 0.045) + 541.67 = 5,562.88 + 541.67 = **6,104.55**/mo → 73,254.60/yr
- Taxable pay = 6,104.55 + 110.83 = 6,215.38/mo → 74,584.56/yr
- Taxable above allowance = 74,584.56 − 11,179 = 63,405.56
- Tax = 20% × 37,700 + 40% × (63,405.56 − 37,700) = 7,540 + 10,282.22 = 17,822.22/yr → 1,485.19/mo
- NI = 8% × (50,270 − 12,570) + 2% × (73,254.60 − 50,270) = 3,016 + 459.69 = 3,475.69/yr → 289.64/mo
- Net = 6,104.55 − 1,485.19 − 289.64 = **4,329.72** vs payslip **4,330.10** (38p; pence rounding in PAYE tables)

## August 2026 (basic £6,153.15/mo)

- Cash pay = 6,153.15 × 0.955 + 541.67 = 5,876.26 + 541.67 = **6,417.93**/mo → 77,015.16/yr
- Taxable pay = 6,417.93 + 110.83 = 6,528.76/mo (matches the payslip's "Taxable pay" exactly) → 78,345.12/yr
- Taxable above allowance = 67,166.12 → tax = 7,540 + 40% × 29,466.12 = 19,326.45/yr → 1,610.54/mo
- NI = 3,016 + 2% × (77,015.16 − 50,270) = 3,016 + 534.90 = 3,550.90/yr → 295.91/mo
- Net = 6,417.93 − 1,610.54 − 295.91 = **4,511.48** vs payslip **4,511.60** (12p)

Both reconcile to within the £1/month tolerance used by the golden test.
