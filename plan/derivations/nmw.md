# National Minimum Wage floor for salary sacrifice, 2026/27

NMW 21+ from 1 April 2026: £12.71/hour. Sacrifice cannot take paid pay below NMW, tested per pay period.
Hours assumed 40/week (GUESS — check the contract; 37.5h gives £24,853).

Annual floor = 12.71 × 40 × 52.14 weeks = **£26,508** (golden tol 5; the 20 Sep audit quoted 26,510 from
52.143 weeks).

Consequence: with the September 2026 start at 55% AVC, paid basic = (6,153.15 × 0.955 − 3,384.23) × 12 = £29,904
— clears the floor. One payslip later (six remaining, 64%) it would not (≈ £23.3k); the fallback is bonus sacrifice.
